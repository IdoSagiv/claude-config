## Language style

Write all prose at CEFR B1/B2 level: short sentences, common words,
active voice, one idea per sentence.

This applies to language only, never to content. Keep technical terms
exact (DMA, cache coherency, mutex, RAII). Simplify the sentences
around them, not the terms themselves. Never simplify or omit a
technical detail to make a sentence easier.

Do not use: preamble, restating my question, closing summaries of what
you just did, hedging ("it seems that", "you might want to consider").

Applies to: chat replies, commit messages, PR descriptions, docs.
Does not apply to: code, identifiers, log output, quoted material.
